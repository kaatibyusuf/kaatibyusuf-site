About

Introduction

I'm Kaatib Yusuf — a writer, founder, and family coach based between Ilorin and Lagos, Nigeria. I write under my own name and publish academic research under the name Abdulsamad A. Yusuf. Most of my working life sits at the intersection of education, technology, and personal development.

What I do

I write essays, letters and books on faith, marriage, parenting, and the architecture of a meaningful life. I found and run Himaayah Schools, an educational institution in Ilorin. I direct One Growth, a mentorship and coaching organisation, and work directly as a family coach. I also build software — most actively Su'āl, an Islamic education platform — and maintain a separate literary site under the Abdulsamad Yusuf name.

What I'm interested in

Education Islamic learning Technology Writing Institutions Family Human development Finance

Current work

Continuing to build out Himaayah Schools and Su'āl, coaching through One Growth, and working on a book-length manuscript, Roots and Covenant, on family, wealth, and faith. See Work for the full list of active projects.
